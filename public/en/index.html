FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few useful things to know.

Quick answers about prices, repair approval, and software.

What does the listed price include?

Prices cover labour. Parts and software licences are charged separately.

Will you start a repair without my approval?

No. For more complex repairs, we confirm the total first and only start after you approve it.

What if my computer won’t power on?

We’ll start with diagnostics. The guide price for a computer with no power or picture is €25.

Do I need to provide a software licence?

Yes. Customers provide licensed software for installation.
